All 4 players play their own ball every hole. The lowest score on each hole wins that hole for the team. Handicaps apply. Flights apply. Most holes won takes the point.

0.5 pts per 18 holes · 1 pt total

2v2 alternate shot across two separate 18-hole matches. First 18: Player A tees off first and on every odd hole. Second 18: Player A tees off on the 2nd tee and every even hole. No handicaps. BLACK tees. Each 18-hole match won earns 0.5 Ryder Cup points.
